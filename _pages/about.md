<div class="home-intro__text">
<p>I am currently a Research Scientist/Engineer at the <a href="https://www.cs.washington.edu/">Paul G. Allen School of Computer Science &amp; Engineering</a> at the University of Washington, working with Prof. <a href="https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~vsiyer/">Vikram Iyer</a>. I joined the <a href="https://ubicomplab.cs.washington.edu/">UbiComp Lab</a>, led by Prof. <a href="https://homes.cs.washington.edu/~shwetak/">Shwetak Patel</a>, during my master's studies and received my M.S. in Electrical and Computer Engineering from the University of Washington. My current work includes a collaboration with General Motors on low-power, multimodal edge sensing for vehicle safety. I also collaborate with Prof. <a href="https://xueqiuyue.com/">Qiuyue (Shirley) Xue</a> at Purdue University and Prof. <a href="https://tingyucheng.com/">Tingyu Cheng</a> at the University of Notre Dame.</p>
<p>I'm broadly interested in ubiquitous computing, wearable sensing, and intelligent embedded systems. I enjoy building end-to-end sensing systems, from hardware design and sensor integration to embedded firmware and on-device machine learning. I'm particularly interested in combining on-device intelligence with ultra-low-power and battery-free computing to make intelligent sensing systems more practical for everyday use.</p>
<p><strong>I am applying to PhD programs for Fall 2027</strong>.</p>
<h2 class="home-intro__heading">Outside the Lab</h2>
<p>Outside the lab, you'll often find me somewhere near the water, scuba diving, freediving, sailing, or surfing. When I'm on land, I enjoy hiking and working out. :)</p>
