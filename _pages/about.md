I am Jiayi (Maggie) Shao. I am affiliated with the [UbiComp Lab](https://ubicomplab.cs.washington.edu/) at the University of Washington, and my background is in Electrical and Computer Engineering.

My research interests are:

- **Wearable and ubiquitous computing** — sensing and interaction that can fit into everyday life
- **On-device machine learning** — learning and inference that run locally, on the device
- **Ultra-low-power and battery-free sensing** — systems designed for severe energy limits

This site is a short record of my research, publications, and CV. Project descriptions and papers will be added as they are ready to share.
